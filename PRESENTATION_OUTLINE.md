**Speaker: Rodrigo**

### Content:
**The Problem:**
- Customers buy one service per visit
- They return weeks/months later for complementary services
- High drop-off risk between visits

**The Solution: "Smart Pathways"**
- Structured multi-visit service plans based on data
- Example: Body Slimming → Toning → Laser (pre-scheduled sequence)

**Why It Works:**
- Customers who buy Slimming are **12x more likely** to buy Toning
- Convert "might return" into "already committed"
- Reduces drop-off, increases predictability

**Revenue Impact:**

- Target: 15% adoption rate (conservative)

### Content:

**Impact:**

## PRESENTATION DESIGN GUIDELINES

### Color Scheme:
- **Primary:** Spa/wellness colors (soft teal, sage green, warm beige)
- **Accents:** Gold for premium, silver for data points
- **Alerts:** Red for problems/gaps, green for solutions/gains

### Typography:
- **Headers:** Bold, sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Poppas)
- **Body:** Clean, readable (e.g., Open Sans, Roboto)
- **Numbers:** Large, bold when showing impact

### Visual Principles:
- **One main idea per slide**
- **Minimal text** - speaker provides details
- **High contrast** for readability
- **Consistent layout** across slides
- **Data visualization over tables** where possible

### Figure Quality:
- All figures from `/outputs/figures/` should be high-resolution
- Crop figures to show only relevant portions
- Add annotations/callouts to guide audience attention
- Use consistent color coding across slides
