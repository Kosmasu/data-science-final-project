## SLIDE 6: Recommendation 4 - Demand-Based Staffing (1.5 minutes)
**Speaker: Kenny**

### Content:
**The Staffing Problem:**
- "Day of Week" is **#1 revenue driver** in our predictive model
- 92% demand variation between Tuesday (low) and Saturday (high)
- Current staffing = flat across all days
- **Result:** Overstaffed Tuesdays, understaffed Saturdays
- Wasted labor costs on slow days + lost revenue on busy days

**Solution: Variable Staffing Using 90-Day Forecast**
- Reduce staff on low-demand days (Mon-Tue-Wed)
- Increase capacity on high-demand days (Fri-Sat-Sun)
- Use our XGBoost model for 90-day demand prediction
- **Data-driven scheduling** matches resources to demand

**Impact:**
- **+88,000 MYR annually**
  - Labor cost savings on slow days
  - Captured revenue on busy days (no more turning customers away)
  - Improved staff satisfaction (better work-life balance)
